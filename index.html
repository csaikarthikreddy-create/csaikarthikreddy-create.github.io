About

I'm passionate about building intelligent systems that combine data, automation, and human creativity. I'm currently pursuing a master's in Artificial Intelligence Systems Management at Carnegie Mellon University, with interests spanning Agentic AI, LLMs, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and Generative AI.

I was raised in a modest family in Chennai, a coastal city in India. As a child, education was a means to independence, growth and recognition in society, and I was a diligent student who prioritised it. From a young age I was drawn to entrepreneurship, a trait that felt genetically imbued — the satisfaction of building things from scratch gave me an adrenaline rush.

At 24, while working as a software developer at Cognizant Technology Solutions, I started my first business — a small 20-seater restaurant/cafe focused on Indo-Asian cuisine. It grew into three outlets across prime locations within five years.

While running that business, I came to believe technology, and AI specifically, was a powerful medium to make a substantial impact on society. I upskilled in Salesforce and joined CRMIT Solutions in 2022, working as a Lead Salesforce Developer on client implementations until I relocated to the United States.

My motto has always been: "Learning is a lifelong journey, and anyone who stops learning moves into oblivion." I made one of the hardest decisions of my life — returning to academia 15 years after completing my undergraduate degree in 2010 — and am now pursuing my master's at Carnegie Mellon.

Featured projects

Meliza Agentic Supply Chain

An agentic AI system for the supply chain that classifies incoming emails into various categories and then autonomously takes necessary actions through agentic workflows. Used a local small LLM model to limit API usage costs

CNN Image Classifier

A deep learning model built with PyTorch that classifies objects across 10 categories with 92% accuracy — data augmentation, learning-rate scheduling, and explainability via feature-map visualization, deployed with FastAPI and Docker.

Personal Research Portal

A baseline RAG pipeline over a corpus of papers on trustworthy RAG evaluation methods, using all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings, sliding-window chunking, FAISS indexing, and a Streamlit front end.

Shop Mini AI Personalization

An AI-powered Shopify Shop Mini app offering personalized product recommendations via Google ADK agents, Arca MCP memory management, and the Shop Minis React SDK.

AI Chatbot Assistant

A contextual chatbot powered by the OpenAI API and LangChain, with a custom React front end, embeddings for long-term memory, and a Node.js backend — deployed on Render with OAuth.

Neural Style Transfer App

An interactive web app that applies artistic styles to photos in real time, built with TensorFlow and a Flask + React front end. Demo: style-transfer-demo.netlify.app.

AI-Powered Fake News Checker

AI agents that check the veracity of a news article, using CrewAI, Google Gemini 2.5, Serper, and Streamlit — each agent assigned a role, task, and tools.
